From the filter to the stars

The LSST Camera's filter changer swaps a filter, the camera is built around it and installed on the Simonyi Survey Telescope, then the light of a star bounces off three mirrors and crosses three lenses and a filter, down to the 3.2-gigapixel focal plane.

Watch it computed live in 3D (the same scene rendered in your browser: needs a good graphics card)

Rendered from the same models and ray tracer as the interactive pages. Filter exchange shown 10× faster than reality; light slowed down to be seen. Not an official Rubin Observatory product.
