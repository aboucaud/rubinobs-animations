Follow the light, step by step

From the summit of Cerro Pachón down to the sensors, in the order the light travels.

1

The observatory & the dome

The summit of Cerro Pachón from day to night. Open the 30 m dome, then start the survey: the telescope hops from field to field while the slower dome turns to keep up.
2

The Simonyi Survey Telescope

Starlight bounces off three mirrors and crosses three lenses before landing on a single CCD. Choose a filter, move the star across the 3.5° field and watch where its light ends up.
3

Inside the LSST Camera

Take apart the largest astronomical camera ever built. Swap filters with the carousel and autochanger, open the shutter, and watch a 30-second exposure build up and read out.
4

The filter exchange system

Five 75 cm filters, a carousel and an autochanger squeezed inside the camera. Watch a complete filter change step by step, with the real timings of the system built by IN2P3/CNRS.

Next: a night of the survey. Slew, settle, expose, read out, repeat: about 1,000 images a night that map the whole southern sky every few nights.
